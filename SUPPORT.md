# vboard Support

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Thank you for using vboard. This page explains how to get help, report issues, or send feedback about the vboard app for Apple Vision Pro.

## Contact

For support, feedback, bug reports, or privacy questions, contact:

vboard-app@artem-shitov.com

## What to Include

When contacting support, please include any details that may help reproduce or understand the issue:

- A short description of what happened.
- What you expected to happen.
- The app version, if available.
- Your visionOS version.
- Whether you were using touch input, a supported spatial stylus, SharePlay, or snapshot export.
- Screenshots or screen recordings, if they are useful and do not contain information you do not want to share.

Please do not send sensitive whiteboard content unless it is necessary for the support request.

## Common Topics

vboard currently supports:

- Drawing with touch input.
- Drawing with supported spatial stylus input (drawing works based on tip pressure detection).
- Erasing strokes.
- Saving whiteboard snapshots to Photos.

If saving a snapshot does not work, check that Photos access is allowed for vboard in system settings.

Make sure that your drawing surface that you put your window against is resistant for damage from your spatial stylus pressure.

If stylus drawing does not behave as expected, include whether the pressure indicator changes, whether the stylus projection appears on the board, and whether touch drawing works.

## Privacy

The current version of vboard is designed as a local-first whiteboard app. For more detail, see the vboard Privacy Policy:

[vboard Privacy Policy](https://github.com/artem-vsh/vboard-app/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

## Response Time

Support requests are reviewed as time permits. Not every feature request can be implemented, but bug reports and reproducible issues are useful and appreciated.
