# vboard Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vboard handles information when you use the vboard app for Apple Vision Pro.

## Summary
 
vboard is a local-first whiteboard app. The app can process whiteboard content, drawing input, and spatial input on your device so that the whiteboard works.

The current version does not include account sign-in, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or developer-operated cloud sync. It also does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

## Information Handled By The App

vboard may process the following information on your device:

- Whiteboard content, including strokes, eraser actions, canvas state, and snapshots.
- Drawing input, including touch input and supported stylus input.
- Spatial input needed to place and interact with the whiteboard in your space.
- App state needed to save, restore, export, or collaborate on a whiteboard.

This processing is used to provide the app's core whiteboard features.

## Information Received By The Developer

The current version of vboard does not automatically send personal data, whiteboard content, analytics events, crash reports, or usage logs to the developer, except for the information provided by Apple by default.

If you contact the developer for support, feedback, or privacy questions, the developer may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message text, screenshots, attachments, and related technical details. That information is used to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, improve the app, and keep appropriate records.

## Local Storage And Exports

vboard may store whiteboard documents, app state, and snapshots locally on your device so that you can continue working later. These files may include the content you draw on the canvas.

When you save or export a snapshot, vboard writes a PNG image to a local Pictures location or another system-provided location. The snapshot remains on your device unless you choose to share, sync, back up, or move it. You control those files through your device, the app, and Apple system settings.

If you use iCloud backup, device migration, file sharing, or other Apple system features, Apple may process or store copies of local app data according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies.

## Permissions

vboard may ask for Apple Vision Pro permissions required for drawing and spatial input:

- World sensing: used to place and interact with the whiteboard in your space.
- Accessory tracking: used to support compatible spatial stylus input.
- Gallery access: user to save whiteboard screenshots.

The current version uses these permissions for app functionality on your device. It does not send world-sensing or accessory-tracking data to a developer-operated server.

## SharePlay And Collaboration

If you use Apple system collaboration features such as SharePlay, whiteboard updates and tool-selection information may be transmitted through Apple's Group Activities infrastructure to participants in the active session.

## Third-Party Services

The current version does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Apple may process information as part of operating Apple Vision Pro, App Store, iCloud, SharePlay, backups, diagnostics, or other Apple services you choose to use. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your Apple settings.

## How Information Is Used

Information handled by vboard is used to:

- Provide drawing, erasing, saving, exporting, and collaboration features.
- Maintain app state and restore your work.
- Respond to support, feedback, or privacy requests if you contact the developer.
- Debug, maintain, and improve the app, including when you choose to provide support information or diagnostic details.

## Your Choices And Controls

You can control information handled by vboard in several ways:

- You can clear whiteboard content or delete saved snapshots from your device.
- You can stop using SharePlay or other collaboration features at any time.
- You can manage app permissions, backups, iCloud settings, and file sharing through Apple system settings.
- You can uninstall the app to remove app-local data, subject to how Apple system backups and synced files are configured on your device.

## Sharing

The current version does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be shared only in the following limited situations:

- You choose to export, save, send, back up, or collaborate using Apple system features or other services on your device.
- You choose to provide information in a support, feedback, or privacy request.
- Disclosure is required to comply with law, protect rights, prevent abuse, or respond to valid legal process.

## Retention

Whiteboard documents, snapshots, and app state stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, remove the app, or manage them through Apple system settings.

Support or privacy correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

## Children's Privacy

vboard is not directed to children under 13. The current version does not knowingly collect personal data from children through developer-operated services.

## Changes To This Policy

vboard may change over time. Future versions may add optional features such as cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, account features, support upload, or developer-operated collaboration services. If those features change how information is collected, used, shared, or retained, this Privacy Policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

The updated policy will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.
